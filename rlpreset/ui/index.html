RL Preset Loader

Pro car designs, straight into your garage

Pick presets from 375 pro players' cars and the app builds them in your Rocket League garage for you, using the items you already own.

Your own presets are never changed: every pro preset goes into a new one.

A few quick questions first. Most answers are filled in for you.

Which platform do you play on?

What resolution is your game at?

Game settings

The app reads the game's menus, so it needs these.

Game language

Switch Rocket League to English (Settings → Interface) before adding presets.

Display mode

Windowed can work, but fullscreen or borderless is more reliable.

While it's working

  • Keep your hands off the mouse and keyboard: the app is using them.
  • The window shrinks to a small bar in the corner. Press Stop there, or hold F8, to stop.
  • Each preset takes about 20 seconds. If you don't own an item, the free one is used instead.
  • Presets you already have are skipped, so nothing gets added twice.

You're set

Next, scan your inventory so the app can show which presets you can fully make. It takes about 3 minutes: start Rocket League, go to the main menu, and keep your hands off the mouse while it runs.

You can always do this later with the Scan inventory button.
