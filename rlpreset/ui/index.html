RL Preset Loader

Pro car designs, straight into your garage

Pick presets from 375 pro players' cars and the app builds them in your Rocket League garage for you, using the items you already own.

This is not an item swapper. It never touches the game's files and never changes your own presets; every pro preset goes into a new one.

What resolution is your game at?

You're set
